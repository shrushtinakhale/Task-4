## Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis Dashboard

### Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using Microsoft Excel and Power BI. The objective is to identify purchasing trends, customer demographics, product performance, seasonal patterns, and customer engagement metrics through an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard transforms raw customer transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support strategic decision-making and improve customer understanding.

## Objectives
. Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
. Identify top-performing product categories.
. Evaluate seasonal sales trends.
. Understand customer demographics.
. Examine subscription and customer loyalty patterns.
. Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

##Key Insights
Product Performance
Clothing category generates the highest revenue.
Accessories contribute significantly to overall sales.
Outerwear generates the lowest revenue.
Customer Demographics
Male customers represent the majority of purchases.
Customer purchasing patterns vary across age groups.
Seasonal Trends
Fall season generates the highest revenue.
Sales remain relatively stable across all seasons.
Subscription Analysis
Non-subscribers contribute a larger share of revenue.
Subscription programs have potential for further growth.
